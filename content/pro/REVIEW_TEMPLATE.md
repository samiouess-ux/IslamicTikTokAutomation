## Non-negotiable rules
- Never invent, paraphrase, silently normalize, or combine religious wording.
- Never infer a hadith grade from a search snippet or an unreferenced badge.
- A secondary compilation or search result is discovery evidence only; open the underlying primary source.
- A `candidate` or `source_checked` record is not approved content.
- Do not set production validator approval flags from this worksheet alone.
- No render, Telegram send, or TikTok publish is authorized by completing this record.
